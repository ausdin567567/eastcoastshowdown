The Showdown

One stage. Two folk traditions.
A decade of the celebration.

East Coast Showdown is the intercollegiate Bhangra and Raas competition hosted at the University of Virginia. What began as a way to bring South Asian folk dance to Charlottesville is now a stage that draws teams from across the country, and for the tenth year, we're throwing it like a shaadi: the garlands, the food, the family, the whole affair.

Punjab · Folk

Bhangra

High-energy Punjabi folk dance built on the dhol. Explosive jumps, tight formations, and boliyan that land like a drop, full-out for eight straight minutes in vibrant phulkari.

DholBoliyanFull energy
Gujarat · Folk

Garba Raas

Gujarati garba-raas danced with dandiya, spinning formations, mirror-work sharp enough to catch the light, and stick-work that has to land in perfect sync across the whole team.

DandiyaFormationsPrecision

More than a trophy

We're dancing for Sakhi

In partnership with

East Coast Showdown is partnering with Sakhi for South Asian Women this year. We aspire to spread cultural awareness through a medium where Bhangra and Garba-Raas are performed and celebrated, and we're just as committed to giving back to the South Asian community.

Sakhi is a nonprofit whose mission is to represent the South Asian diaspora in a survivor-led movement for gender justice, honoring the collective and inherent power of all survivors of violence. They serve survivors through a combination of efforts including, but not limited to, direct services, advocacy and organizing, technical assistance, and community outreach.

Over the past six years, ECS has donated more than $10,000 to South Asian charities. This upcoming year, we're committing 100% of our proceeds (the net total after operating expenses) to Sakhi's mission.
